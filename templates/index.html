How It Works (School Viva & Presentation Guide)

1. YOLOv8 Detection

The system passes the image into a pretrained YOLOv8 Nano neural network trained on the international RDD2022 (Road Damage Dataset). It identifies bounding boxes for Potholes, Longitudinal Cracks, Transverse Cracks, and Alligator Cracks in a single forward pass.

2. Relative Severity Metric

Damage severity is determined by the bounding box area relative to the whole image: Low (<5%), Medium (5%–15%), and High (>15%). Potholes are automatically escalated by one tier because of their sudden impact risk.

3. Road Condition Grading

Overall road state is categorized as Good (0 defects), Fair (only low severity cracks), Poor (at least one medium defect), or Critical (any high severity damage or 5+ potholes).
